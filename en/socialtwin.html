SocialTwin's virtual population is not a set of made-up AI profiles. It is high-precision synthetic data that reproduces the demographic, social and lifestyle structure of South Korea from official national statistics published by Statistics Korea and other public data sources. Both the database and the simulation software that runs it are registered with the Korea Copyright Commission.
