03 · Technology & Trust

Four pillars of a
trustworthy virtual society.

Technology is the basis of trust.
OmniNode earns trust in its virtual society through representativeness · context · validation · quality control.
Our virtual population reaches 95% statistical consistency and over 90% agreement with human responses.

TRUST · 01
Representa­tiveness
CA-IPF
"A virtual society that mirrors the real population as closely as possible"

A synthetic population that preserves the correlations between demographic axes recreates the structure of South Korea's real population. We run a nationwide simulated population of 50,000 people (1:1,000 scale).

#CA-IPF #SyntheticPopulation #StatisticsKorea
TRUST · 02
Context
LLM Persona
"Virtual people with social conditions and everyday context"

Beyond age, region and income, we use language models to build virtual people with everyday context, social conditions and values — designed to respond consistently with that context.

#LLM Persona #Memory Stream
TRUST · 03
Validation
Validation
"Validated against real-world data"

We validate the virtual population's responses against real human responses. 94.5% distribution consistency, 89.5% rank consistency

#MAE #DistributionFit #Spearman ρ #RankFit
TRUST · 04
Quality control
Quality Management
"Managing the quality of surveys and responses together"

We continuously check that AI-generated surveys and virtual-panel responses can be trusted, managing instrument reliability and validity, response consistency, bias and variance.

#Reliability·Validity #BiasChecks

04 · Vision & Principles

Building decisions
that AI and people make together.

Four principles that shape how OmniNode sees the relationship between AI and people.

PRINCIPLE · 01

AI and people

AI does not replace people. It is a tool that helps people ask deeper questions.

PRINCIPLE · 02

Data and people

Data is not a way to simplify people — it is the starting point for understanding their context more precisely.

PRINCIPLE · 03

The role of simulation

Simulation does not make real-world decisions for you. It is an advance experiment that lowers the cost of real-world failure.

PRINCIPLE · 04

Social responsibility

AI-based predictions must be verifiable, and used with respect for people and society.
